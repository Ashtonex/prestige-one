1. What is Prestige ONE & How It Works

Prestige ONE is a mobile-first, enterprise-grade Progressive Web Application (PWA) purpose-built for premium international academies. Developed for FLECTERE, it unifies student admissions, classroom registers, academic reporting, and tuition billing into a single authoritative digital platform.

🏛️ The Fictional Demonstration Academy

Prestige International Academy
• Location: Sommerschield Executive Quarter, Maputo
• Academic Year: 2026 (Term 1 Active)
• Base Currency: Mozambican Metical (MZN)
• Live Class Cohorts: Grade 7A, 7B, and 8A
• Mission: Providing seamless digital continuity across leadership, teachers, parents, and students.

⚡ Core Technological Pillars

• Zero Mock Data: Native PostgreSQL database with strict relational constraints and audit logs.
• Persistent App Shell: Header, sidebar, and mobile nav survive internal client-side navigation without unmounting.
• Zero-Data-Loss PWA: Custom service worker precaches offline fallbacks while ensuring authenticated data is never unsafely cached.
• Dual-Screen Fluidity: Fully responsive across 375px mobile phones, tablets, and wide executive dashboards.

2. Platform Architecture & Data Flow

C. Progressive Web App (PWA) Offline Reserve Mechanism

🛡️ Client Continuity & Integrity Guarantee:
Prestige ONE incorporates an intelligent Service Worker (sw.js). If internet connectivity drops during school operations:
  1. The persistent header immediately updates the network pill from ● Online to ● Offline Mode.
  2. Cached static assets and previously loaded screens remain responsive without blank white screens.
  3. New navigations gracefully serve the Prestige ONE Offline Reserve Screen (/~offline) informing the user that data edits require an authoritative PostgreSQL connection.
  4. When connection restores, the status pill auto-recovers to Online without losing local page state.

4. Step-by-Step Demonstration Walkthrough

Follow this exact 12-step sequence to deliver a flawless client demonstration of the primary workflow: Student Enrollment and Institutional Record Synchronization.

  • Step 1
    Sign In as Administrator: Visit http://localhost:3000/login. Click the "Admin" button to fill admin@prestige.co.mz / PrestigeAdmin2026!. Click Sign In.
  • Step 2
    Inspect Executive Dashboard: Arrive at /dashboard. Point out the live metrics: Active Students (6), Term Attendance (94.2%), Tuition Collected (82.5% MZN).
  • Step 3
    Open Student Directory: Click Students in the sidebar or bottom mobile nav (/students). Notice instant client-side transition without page reload. Filter or search existing students.
  • Step 4
    Initiate Enrollment: Click "+ Enroll New Student" button to navigate to /students/new.
  • Step 5
    Input Student Details: Enter Admission No (e.g., PIA-2026-099), First Name (Zayd), Last Name (Mabunda), Date of Birth, Gender, Select Class Section: Grade 7A.
  • Step 6
    Input Guardian & Medical Profile: Enter Guardian Name (Salimo Mabunda), Email (salimo.m@example.com), Phone (+258 84 900 1122), and Medical Notes (No known allergies).
  • Step 7
    Submit Form with Concurrency Lock: Click "Complete Student Enrollment". Observe the button immediately disable to prevent duplicate submissions, followed by a green toast confirmation.
  • Step 8
    Verify Master Profile: You are redirected to /students/[id]. Highlight the generated student banner, emergency phone, residential address, and active class enrollment.
  • Step 9
    Cross-Section Verification: Click Classes in navigation, then open Grade 7A (/classes/[id]). The new student appears directly in the section roster with full details!
  • Step 10
    Edit Student Record: Return to the student profile, click "Edit Record" (/students/[id]/edit). Modify Emergency Phone to +258 84 999 8877 and click Save Changes.
  • Step 11
    Verify Synchronization: Confirm the updated phone number renders immediately on both the student profile and the class roster.
  • Step 12
    Prove Durable Persistence: Perform a hard browser reload (Ctrl + R) or log out and log back in. The record is permanently preserved in PostgreSQL!
